Shape an electric well with electrodes

How can stationary pieces of metal carry an ion around a bend? Read the racetrack drawing, then trace the voltage command that shifts a well.

Read the track from the side

The orange dot in panel c is above the electrode surface. Rapidly alternating voltages provide confinement transverse to the path. Slower voltages on nearby metal segments shape the well along it. Increasing one electrode alone can change both the depth and the centre. A transport waveform is a coordinated sequence for several electrodes.

Follow one moving minimum

Imagine progressively raising the floor on the left and lowering it on the right. The ion feels a force toward the new minimum. Moving too abruptly can excite vibration even when the ion reaches the correct destination. In the H2 conveyor section, repeated groups of electrodes share signals. Several wells can move together. Ask which other wells share a channel before treating one move as independent.
