Find the first pair

Quantinuum’s original H2 benchmark prepares a shared state across 32 ions. It begins with a superposition on ion 0, then entangles pairs in a branching tree. The released circuit first pairs q0 with q1. In the QuantumOS compiled replay the ions meet at DG01. Open the first twelve control cycles and inspect the laser pulse associated with that pair. The controller executes a reconstructed program on the RISC-Q hardware model. Inspect the first gate in the workspace.

Change the measurement question

After preparing the state, the experimenters rotate the measurement basis for every ion. For each shot, count the 1s in its 32-bit result. An even count contributes +1, an odd count −1. Average over shots at the same angle, then repeat at the next angle. A coherent 32-ion superposition produces a parity that varies as cos(32θ) in the ideal sequence. A mixture can share its populations while losing this phase dependence. Compare the 32 phase settings with 1,600 hardware shots.
