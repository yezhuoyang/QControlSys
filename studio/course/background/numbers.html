Numbers and sources

Every latency, duration, rate and size the simulator uses, in one place, with its unit, its source and its status. Use this page to check a number you see in a run, or to find what to change when you want the simulator to model a different machine.

Values were read from the files named in each table: riscq/soc.mjs (model(), MAP and the constants), QControl/RiscQ/Timing.lean, riscq/firmware.mjs, riscq/standards.mjs, riscq/platforms/*.mjs, validation/riscq/x6y3.json, validation/riscq/x6y3-calibration.json, validation/neutral/zac/full_architecture_2aod.json and machine/interconnect.mjs. A run also lists the values it used in program.model and machine.provenance.

1How to read the status column

StatusMeaning
citedStated by the named document, paper or configuration file, used as stated.
derivedArithmetic on cited numbers. The arithmetic is shown.
compositeCombines numbers from different machines because no one source states them all.
assumedA model choice, within a documented range where one exists. Change it and the results change.
referenceListed for comparison only; the simulator does not use it in a run.

"Cited" refers to RISC-Q documents by their file names in the RISC-Q repository, as riscq/soc.mjs names them. A cited RISC-Q number is a statement of RISC-Q's design documents, not a measurement of a board.

One Studio time unit of transport is one intrazone shift of 58 µs plus Pino et al.'s 10% overhead, 63.8 µs. Longer transports are whole multiples. The run states this in machine.provenance.time.

What to remember

  1. Most controller latencies are cited from RISC-Q's documents; three are assumed: the core time lag, the report path and the core penalties.
  2. The leads 34, 35, 36, 52, 11 and 2 are derived from cited block latencies, and the derivation is proved.
  3. The transmon ring is RISC-Q's x6y3 build and calibration; the ion and atom controller clocks and sizes are this project's choices.
  4. Ion durations come from Pino et al. 2021; atom durations from the ZAC program; photon statistics are a composite.
  5. A run lists the values it used in program.model and machine.provenance.
