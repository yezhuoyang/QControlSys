The QuantumOS course

Learn how a quantum computer is controlled.

A quantum program does nothing until classical hardware turns it into pulses, waveforms and laser light at the right nanosecond. This course follows one path: the stack of a real machine, the controller that schedules it, the three platforms it drives, the hardware you can design yourself, and what fault tolerance asks of it. Every exercise is checked against the simulator, running the same firmware the lesson describes.
