The team prepares pairs of rubidium-87 atoms separated by 3 µm. Figure 1b shows the larger separation of 110 µm after a 300 µs transport interval. The static array uses 830 nm trapping light, and mobile tweezers use 828 nm. A two-channel Spectrum M4i.6631-x8 produces signals for crossed deflectors. The 795 nm Raman system drives the qubit. These wavelengths refer to different jobs on the same apparatus. Bluvstein et al., Methods.

Move the wells while protecting the phase

The authors interpolate positions with a cubic profile. Here the two ends of a representative pair move symmetrically about their midpoint and start and finish at rest. That choice fixes the separation to a + (b − a)(3s² − 2s³), where s is elapsed time divided by duration. The atoms follow the spots ideally. The eight X and Y rotations reverse the accumulation of certain unwanted phase shifts. Their evenly spaced midpoints are a reconstruction. At the reported 1 MHz rotation frequency a π pulse takes 0.5 µs.
