# Physical validation and the three design tools

The [validation bench](http://127.0.0.1:7432/validation) compares an observable from an executed waveform program with an observable from an independent experiment. A case records the programmed setting, source row or plotted marker, calibration split, unit and program hash. The controller produces the samples. The device and readout models consume those samples. The scorer uses held-out measurements. Edited waveforms run as counterfactuals and do not inherit a measurement from an earlier setting.

## Scientific basis

**Superconducting circuits.** Quantum Metal, originally Qiskit Metal, provides parameterized chip geometry. The native TransmonPocket model supplies mask polarity, lengths, junction centerlines and pins. Energy-participation quantization connects electromagnetic modes and junction participation to a circuit Hamiltonian. Chip polygons alone do not supply a microwave transfer function, calibrated drive strength, anharmonicity or receiver gain. The studio therefore uses actual Metal geometry for the design view and a separately calibrated transmon for the measured response. A device-specific profile could ingest independently measured resonance and loss parameters or a solver’s mode and participation data. [Quantum Metal](https://github.com/qiskit-community/qiskit-metal) · [Minev et al., energy-participation quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00620)

Labranca et al. drive a transmon with a Gaussian microwave pulse, collect the field it emits, split it between two receiver chains and analyze their mode-matched cross-power. The implemented model demodulates the actual delivered 50 MHz IF samples, integrates the complex envelope, and maps resonant pulse area to excitation with a fitted offset and contrast for detection. This tests a signed detector observable in the deposited units. It does not infer photon-counting efficiency from microwave voltage. The measured slice fixes pulse width and varies amplitude. IQ imbalance, frequency-dependent attenuation, finite relaxation during the pulse, leakage and drift would need additional independent sweeps. [Labranca et al.](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ade736) · [Original MATLAB data](https://doi.org/10.17632/vzs49gddsm.1) · [Benchmark protocol](../validation/superconducting/protocol.md)

**Neutral atoms.** ZAC’s ZAIR instructions describe storage sites, activation, shared AOD movement and deactivation in a zoned architecture. A native move supplies a requested displacement and its schedule. To realize it optically, an apparatus needs a map from RF frequency to trap position, a timing model for the deflector and a model for pickup and release. For a single stationary acoustic tone, the angular deflection scales with laser wavelength divided by acoustic velocity. Relay magnification and objective focal length map that angle into sample-plane displacement. Camera orientation determines its sign. [Lin, Tan and Cong, ZAC architecture and instruction model](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.11784v1) · [ZAC compiler and input files](https://github.com/UCLA-VAST/ZAC)

Brooks et al. report the AOD, objective, relay, 625 MS/s control hardware and fitted positions of individual Cs atoms. The simulator synthesizes a single tone at that rate and estimates its frequency from delivered voltage samples and their exact picosecond timestamps. Six measured points set the slope and offset. Five different frequencies test the prediction. For the separate ZAC example, the nominal optics prescribe the drive for its X movement, and the measured slope predicts the achieved displacement. Y-axis calibration, survival during acceleration, acoustic transients, and losses during handoff are separate observables. Gate fidelity also needs interaction and phase measurements. [Brooks et al.](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ac0000) · [Camera data](https://doi.org/10.15128/r1k3569440m) · [Evered et al., independently calibrated neutral-atom gates](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06481-y)

**Trapped ions.** QCCD Studio checks transport sites and gate order. The H2 reproduction binds an actual source instruction to its transport group and traversed sites. Its architectural time units do not determine a pulse shape in volts. The separate Clark experiment gives a 120 µm command, an update interval and an optically inferred ion position versus delay. We tested the instantaneous well-following approximation after averaging over the 200 ns probe. Its error on the fast shuttle means it cannot support a position tolerance of 12 µm in this regime. A prospective improvement is to identify the electrode filter on an independent capture, use a voltage-to-potential basis for the actual trap, and integrate the ion equation of motion before applying the optical measurement model. [QCCD Studio H2](https://www.qccd.academy/reproduce/moses2023/) · [Moses et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.13.041052) · [Clark et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.107.043119)

Walther et al. show why endpoint arrival alone is insufficient. In a segmented RF Paul trap, the dwell between forward and reverse ramps determines the phase at which coherent displacements add. The simulator reconstructs both 11.2 µs ramps on a 20 ns issue clock, delivers 28 DAC updates per direction, and evaluates both complex displacements at the published 1.41 MHz axial frequency. Seven held-out sideband-thermometry measurements test the residual phonon number. A different experiment by Jain et al. tests a slower regime on a Penning microtrap. Its observable is the bright-state population after transport. Agreement with stationary-control spectroscopy at that endpoint cannot validate the instantaneous trajectory of an RF-trapped ion or the timing of H2. [Walther et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.0364) · [Jain et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07111-x)

Quantinuum also released the individual shot strings and matching circuits for the 32-ion H2 GHZ analysis scan. Each case reconstructs the two analysis-angle words on the controller. The measured signed parity is the mean of +1 for an even number of detections and −1 for an odd number. The response is an offset plus an in-phase contrast times cos(32θ). The sampled angles are extrema of that cosine, so this scan cannot identify the sine quadrature or a continuous phase offset. Both signs occur in each split. Sixteen phase settings calibrated the model and the remaining sixteen tested it on 800 shots. A virtual Z rotation is a frame change. The microvolt numbers in this view encode delivered register words and do not represent measured laser voltages. The Studio replay opens the native program for the selected archival circuit. [Matched H2 shots and QASM](https://github.com/Quantinuum/quantinuum-hardware-h2-benchmark/blob/d422a716977899d7d6603c9efadb44e89e495125/ghz/data/GHZ_N32.json)

## Held-out results

| Hardware and observable | Held-out settings | Measured error | Registered limit |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| H2 GHZ signed parity | 16 | 0.0852 RMS | ≤0.15 |
| Cs position under RF steering | 5 | 0.0504 µm RMS | ≤0.25 µm |
| Transmon emission cross-power | 25 | 6.12% of experimental range, R² 0.9585 | ≤15% and R² ≥0.75 |
| Calcium ion position on the 1.2 µs shuttle | 150 | 19.895 µm RMS | **Missed** ≤12 µm |
| Calcium ion excitation after paired Paul-trap shuttles | 7 | 10.4405 phonons RMS | **Missed** ≤10 phonons |
| Beryllium bright population following slow Penning-trap transport | 201 | 0.08095 RMS, maximum absolute error 0.293 | ≤0.10 RMS |

Each limit applies to the stated aggregate observable. For example, the largest H2 phase residual was 0.185. All the reported holdouts are settings or conditions in the named experiments. They do not quantify transfer to a newly fabricated chip or a later calibration day.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, build with `lake build` and use Python 3.10 or later. The reference datasets used by the runner are checked in. Re-downloading original source archives or re-exporting Metal geometry is optional and uses the tools described in each protocol.

| Command | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `python -B -X utf8 validation/run.py --write` | Re-run all programs, fit each on calibration observations, score held-out observations, and atomically write the studio report. |
| `python -B -X utf8 validation/run.py --platform neutral --check` | Print a fresh report for that hardware and return failure if a registered criterion is missed. |
| `python -B -X utf8 validation/run.py --experiment qccd-pair --check` | Reproduce the RF transport energy check, including its missed criterion. |
| `python -B -X utf8 validation/witness.py` | Independently recompute held-out scores with exact rational arithmetic from the archived decimal values. |
| `python -B -X utf8 validation/run.py --verify-case < exported-case.json` | Recompute an exported case, including physical part mappings and the first differing controller or analysis step. |
| `python -B validation/neutral/zac_adapter.py` | Replay the pinned native ZAC source and recompute the X-displacement projection. |
| `python -B validation/qccd/verify_design.py` | Re-run the native H2 design witness against the local QCCD Studio checkout. |

The website serves the [checked-in report](../validation/results.json) and executes the selected case through the same native controller. Agents can read `GET /api/validation/results` and post `{"platform":"neutral","case":"durham-fig4a-row-1"}` to `/api/validation/replay`. An optional `experiment` selects an additional study for the platform. An optional `program` supplies a counterfactual. The reply includes physical component IDs, the actual controller trace and the subsequent offline calculations. Physical timestamps apply to the reconstructed program. An offline comparison carries no invented laboratory acquisition time.

The Lean checker independently verifies the decision rule on the supplied held-out records. It checks unique IDs, separate calibration and held-out sets, and the registered thresholds. That theorem and the software conformance checks do not measure electronics. A board-specific digital twin still needs synchronized command, bus or trigger, analog-output and device-readout captures from the same identified installation, plus a second operating condition to test any fit. [Controller model](../QControl/Controller.lean) · [Validation checker](../QControl/Validation.lean) · [NIST on validating digital twins for an intended use](https://www.nist.gov/publications/credibility-consideration-digital-twins-manufacturing)

Each of the 32 archival H2 circuits has an independent Studio compilation, a hash of the original QASM and a hash of the compiled program. Each replay expanded to 1,170 native cycles, cleared the routing and ordering certificate, and matched the analysis operations on all 32 ions. Studio reports its full-unitary check as partial.
